Ghibli Collection No. 14

Spirited
Away

千と千尋の神隠し

movie_filter Hayao Miyazaki
2001
2h 5m

A young girl, Chihiro, becomes trapped in a strange new world of spirits. When her parents undergo a mysterious transformation, she must call upon the courage she never knew she had to free her family.

Wandering into a world ruled by gods, witches, and spirits, a young girl transforms. Spirited Away tells the story of Chihiro Ogino, a sullen ten-year-old girl who, while moving to a new neighborhood, enters the world of Kami (spirits) of Japanese Shinto folklore.

Ghibli Collection No. 11

Princess
Mononoke

もののけ姫

movie_filter Hayao Miyazaki
1997
2h 14m

On a journey to find the cure for a Tatarigami's curse, Ashitaka finds himself in the middle of a war between the forest gods and Tatara, a mining colony. In this quest he also meets San, the Mononoke Hime.

A landmark in the world of animation, Princess Mononoke is a complex tale of the struggle between nature and industrialization. It weaves a tapestry of myths and moral ambiguity where there are no clear villains, only conflicting needs.

Ghibli Collection No. 04

My Neighbor
Totoro

となりのトトロ

movie_filter Hayao Miyazaki
1988
1h 26m

When two girls move to the country to be near their ailing mother, they have adventures with the wondrous forest spirits who live nearby.

A gentle and heartwarming tale that captures the innocence of childhood. Totoro has become a cultural icon, representing the magic that can be found in nature and everyday life.
